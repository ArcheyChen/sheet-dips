Two windings of a spiral fit of PHerc. 0306B, z 8784-10212 (volume 20250521133212, 8.64 µm). They were fitted with
`fit_spiral` (ScrollPrize/villa spiral-fitting) and snapped onto the m7 surface prediction. They are tifxyz meshes at
20 canvas px per grid node, in level-0 voxel coordinates.
- w076: has three sheet-switch islands in rows 0-1000, columns 200-2000 (README, result 1).
- w077: the next winding, a good surface (94 % on-sheet); kept as a reference (the controlled test now runs on the
  306B winners' published region mesh, which carries their text; README, result 2).
